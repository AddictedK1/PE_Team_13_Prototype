PERSONAL INFORMATION:

The decision and scores must be based only on job-relevant information.

Do not use or infer personal or protected characteristics, including:

- Gender
- Age
- Race
- Ethnicity
- Religion
- Caste
- Disability
- Marital status
- Sexual orientation
- Health information
- Photograph
- Physical appearance
- Other demographic characteristics

Do not use irrelevant personal information when evaluating the candidate.

Do not infer qualifications or abilities that are not supported by the resume.
